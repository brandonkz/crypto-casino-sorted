1. Instant Rakeback, up to 70%

Rakeback returns a percentage of the house edge on eligible bets, not a percentage of the full bet amount. That distinction matters because a 70% rakeback rate does not mean you get 70% of every bet back.

You bet $200 on a game with a 5% house edge.
The house's expected take is $10.
At 70% rakeback, $7 comes back to you.
At 20% rakeback, $2 comes back to you.

The best part is structural: rakeback is ongoing and has no classic 35x wagering trap. It is not a locked bonus path built around breakage. It is reward balance you can claim from the dashboard.
