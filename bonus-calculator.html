How This Works

Expected Value (EV) = Bonus Amount - (Total Wagering Requirement x House Edge)

Most bonuses are -EV (negative expected value) because the wagering requirement forces you to risk more than the bonus is worth. A 40x wagering requirement on a $100 bonus means you must wager $4,000. At 4% house edge (96% RTP slots), you'll lose ~$160 on average to clear a $100 bonus.

Only take bonuses with +EV or close to breakeven. Casinos know most players won't do this math — that's how they profit.
