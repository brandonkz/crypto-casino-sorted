Responsible Gambling

Updated 2026-07-03

CryptoCasinoSorted is for adults only. You must be 18+ and legally allowed to gamble in your jurisdiction.

Get help

If gambling is causing stress, debt, secrecy or loss of control, stop playing and speak to a support organisation. Useful resources include BeGambleAware and GamCare.

Self-exclusion

Use account limits, cooling-off periods and self-exclusion tools offered by gambling operators. If you need a broader block, look into national self-exclusion systems available in your country.

Crypto-specific risk

Crypto gambling adds volatility, wallet mistakes, network-fee risk and limited chargeback options. Never gamble with borrowed money, emergency funds or funds you cannot afford to lose.
