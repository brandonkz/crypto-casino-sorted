House Edge & Transparency

This is where crypto casinos should separate themselves from traditional online casinos, and both Stake and Roobet do a decent job.

Stake publishes the house edge on all their original games. Dice is 1%. Plinko varies by row count and risk level (typically 1-3%). Crash is 1%. This transparency is genuinely good — you know exactly what you're paying to play, which is more than most traditional casinos offer.

Roobet is similarly transparent on their originals. Crash runs at a 3% house edge. Their other originals vary but are generally in the 1-4% range. Slightly higher than Stake's on average, but still reasonable by industry standards.

For third-party slots, the house edge is determined by the game provider (Pragmatic Play, Hacksaw, etc.), not the casino.
