Bonuses and VIP Programs

Two very different philosophies here.

Roobet runs a traditional tiered VIP program. You start with basic rakeback and unlock better rates as you wager more. Higher tiers get access to dedicated VIP managers, exclusive promotions, and better rakeback percentages. Roobet also runs regular leaderboard competitions and giveaways through their Twitch partnerships. The VIP ceiling is high — if you're wagering serious volume, the rewards can be substantial.

Rollbit does things differently with the RLB token. Stake RLB to earn lossback and other benefits. The more RLB you hold and stake, the better your rewards. This creates an interesting dynamic where your "VIP status" is tied to token ownership rather than just wagering volume. It's a more crypto-native approach — and if RLB appreciates in value, your staking rewards compound.

The flip side: if RLB drops in value, your VIP benefits are worth less. It's a bet on the platform's token economics alongside your gambling. Roobet's traditional model is more predictable. Neither approach is objectively better — it depends on whether you want straightforward rakeback or a token-based ecosystem.
