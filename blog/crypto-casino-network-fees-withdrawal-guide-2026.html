USDT TRC20: usually the cleanest stablecoin option

USDT on Tron is popular because the value stays in dollars and fees are usually low enough that small withdrawals still make sense. If you want to cash out $200 without worrying about Bitcoin moving 2% before it hits your exchange, TRC20 is often the sensible choice.

The risk is user error. USDT exists on multiple networks. TRC20 is Tron. ERC20 is Ethereum. BEP20 is BNB Chain. If your casino sends TRC20 and your exchange deposit address is ERC20-only, you may have created a support ticket instead of a withdrawal. Always match the network on both sides before confirming.

TRC20 is best for players who want stable value, low fees and a clean path back to an exchange. It is less ideal if your local exchange does not support Tron deposits or adds manual review friction to stablecoin deposits.

Litecoin: boring, useful, underrated

Litecoin remains one of the most practical gambling rails because it is fast, cheap and widely supported. It does not have the hype of Solana or the prestige of Bitcoin, but for a $50 to $500 casino withdrawal, boring is good.

The trade-off is volatility. LTC can move while you are withdrawing, especially if you leave it sitting in a wallet overnight. If you use Litecoin as a rail, treat it as transport, not a portfolio decision. Withdraw, confirm, send to exchange if needed, and convert when that is your plan.

Litecoin also works well for testing a casino before depositing bigger. Our withdrawal test guide explains the small-cashout routine: deposit a modest amount, place a few normal bets, withdraw, and see what happens before you trust the cashier with serious money.

Solana: fast, cheap, but check support first

Solana withdrawals can feel almost instant when everything works. Fees are tiny, confirmations are quick and the user experience is excellent on modern wallets. For casinos that support SOL properly, it is one of the best rails in 2026.

The catch is operational support. Some casinos support SOL deposits but not all tokens on Solana. Some exchanges pause SOL deposits during maintenance. Some users send to the wrong address type or forget that a platform may require a specific deposit flow. Before using SOL for a large casino cashout, test with a small withdrawal first.

Bitcoin and Ethereum: good brands, not always good rails

Bitcoin is universally recognised and widely supported, which makes it comforting for larger withdrawals. But for small withdrawals, the fee and confirmation time can be annoying. If the network is busy, a small BTC cashout can feel slow even when the casino approved it quickly.

Ethereum mainnet is worse for small withdrawals. ETH makes sense if you are already operating on mainnet or withdrawing a large enough balance that gas is noise. For a $75 casino balance, Ethereum is usually the wrong tool unless the fee is subsidised or you have a specific reason to keep ETH.
