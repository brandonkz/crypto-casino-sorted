Provably Fair Games

House Edge

House edge varies by game type on both platforms, so blanket comparisons are tricky. Here's the general picture:

Stake Originals run house edges between 1% and 4% depending on the game and settings. Dice is around 1%, Crash is roughly 1%, and games like Mines vary based on how many mines you select. These are some of the lowest house edges you'll find anywhere in online gambling.

Third-party slots on both platforms have similar RTPs since they come from the same providers. Pragmatic Play slots, for example, have the same RTP on Stake as they do on Shuffle. Expect 94-97% RTP on most slots, which translates to a 3-6% house edge.

Shuffle's competitive positioning emphasizes transparency about house edges and RTPs. They don't have proprietary games with independently verifiable edges, but the third-party games they host are standard across the industry.

Bottom line: Stake's Originals offer some of the best house edges in the industry. For everything else, the two platforms are essentially equal since they use the same providers.
