Game Selection

Stake

Stake has one of the largest game libraries in crypto gambling. Over 3,000 titles from major providers like Pragmatic Play, Evolution, Hacksaw, and Push Gaming. Their Stake Originals — Dice, Plinko, Mines, Crash, Limbo — are provably fair and have become some of the most popular games in the industry.

The sports betting section is comprehensive too, covering everything from Premier League to esports with competitive odds.

Rollbit

Rollbit has a smaller game library (~1,000 slots and table games) but makes up for it with unique offerings. Their Rollbit X crypto futures trading lets you leverage crypto positions up to 1000x. They've also integrated NFT trading — you can buy, sell, and even gamble with NFTs directly on the platform.

If you're purely a slot player, Stake has more variety. If you want a crypto-native experience that combines gambling, trading, and NFTs, Rollbit is more interesting.

🏆 Winner: Stake for pure game variety. Rollbit if you want trading + gambling in one platform.

Withdrawal Speed

Both casinos process withdrawals quickly, but Stake has a slight edge based on our withdrawal speed testing:

  • Rollbit: ~1 minute internal processing, 3-7 minutes to wallet

Both are genuinely "instant" by any reasonable standard. Neither should give you withdrawal anxiety.

VIP Programs & Rakeback

Stake VIP

Stake's VIP program is widely considered the best in crypto gambling. Benefits include:

  • Rakeback (percentage of house edge returned)
  • Weekly and monthly bonuses based on play volume
  • Personal VIP host at higher tiers
  • Reload bonuses and surprise drops
"Stake VIP is the main reason I stay. The rakeback alone makes the house edge almost zero on originals." — r/Stake_com

Rollbit VIP

Rollbit's VIP program ties into their RLB token. Higher levels unlock better rakeback and access to exclusive features. Holding and staking RLB can boost your rewards. It's a solid program, but less generous than Stake for pure gamblers.

The difference: Rollbit rewards you for being part of their ecosystem (holding tokens, trading). Stake rewards you purely based on gambling volume.

🏆 Winner: Stake for gamblers. Rollbit if you're deep in the crypto/NFT ecosystem.

KYC & Privacy

Both casinos operate on a threshold-based KYC model:

  • Small deposits and withdrawals: no ID required
  • Larger amounts or suspicious activity: KYC may be triggered
