How to Test XTP Safely

Do not start by claiming the biggest deposit match. Bonuses add rules: wagering, game weighting, max bet limits, expiry windows and possible max cashout caps. If your goal is to answer "is XTP legit", a bonus makes the first test messy. Prove the basic withdrawal first, then test the promo later.

What Would Be a Red Flag?

  • Support cannot explain withdrawal limits or KYC thresholds clearly.
  • Your account shows a reward but the terms are hard to find.
  • A small no-bonus withdrawal gets delayed without a clear reason.
  • Bonus rules mention max bet or max cashout limits you cannot easily follow.
  • The casino encourages larger deposits before you have completed a withdrawal.

One red flag does not automatically prove a casino is illegitimate, but it is enough to keep your balance small. The goal is not to win an argument with support. The goal is to avoid leaving money somewhere that has not earned it yet.
