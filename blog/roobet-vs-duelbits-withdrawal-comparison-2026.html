No Bonus First, Always

Bonuses make withdrawal comparisons messy. A casino can pay normal balances quickly while enforcing strict bonus rules on promotional balances. If your first cashout is attached to wagering, max-bet rules or restricted games, you will not know whether the cashier is slow or whether you created a terms problem.

Before taking any Roobet or Duelbits offer, use the bonus calculator. Add the deposit, match, wagering requirement and realistic average odds or game contribution. If the promo forces you to play longer than planned, it is entertainment spend, not value. That is fine if you know it upfront. It is expensive if you learn it while trying to withdraw.

Use the provably fair verifier and the audit checklist before trusting a game because it has a crypto logo beside it.

Important: provably fair is not a withdrawal guarantee. Fair game math and cashier reliability are two separate checks.

KYC and Region Risk

No-KYC claims are not permanent immunity from verification. Crypto casinos usually reserve the right to request documents for suspicious activity, restricted jurisdictions, large withdrawals, duplicate accounts, bonus abuse or source-of-funds reviews. Roobet and Duelbits should both be approached with that assumption.

Read the terms before depositing, especially if you use a VPN, travel often, live in a restricted region or plan to keep a larger balance. If you would not pass a future verification request, do not leave money on site that would hurt to have frozen. Your wallet is the storage layer. The casino account is only a session tool.
