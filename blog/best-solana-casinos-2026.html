What to Watch Out For

Not every casino that "supports Solana" gives you the full benefit:

  • Wrapped SOL vs. native SOL — Some casinos convert your SOL to an internal balance. This doesn't affect gameplay but can add an extra conversion step (and fee) on withdrawal.
  • Minimum withdrawals — A few casinos set minimum SOL withdrawals higher than necessary. Check before depositing.
  • Network congestion — Solana has historically experienced outages and congestion. In 2026, network stability has improved significantly, but it's not perfect.
  • SOL price swings — Solana is more volatile than Bitcoin. A 10% price drop while you're playing means your withdrawal is worth less in USD terms.
