FAQ

Is Litecoin safe for casino deposits?

Yes. Litecoin uses the same proof-of-work security model as Bitcoin and has been running since 2011 without any major security incidents. The blockchain is secure, and transactions are irreversible once confirmed. The risk isn't in Litecoin itself — it's in choosing a reputable casino.

Can I withdraw in Litecoin if I deposited in Bitcoin?

Most crypto casinos allow you to withdraw in any supported cryptocurrency, regardless of what you deposited. So yes, you can deposit BTC and withdraw LTC (to save on withdrawal fees), or vice versa. Check the specific casino's policies.

What's the minimum Litecoin deposit at most casinos?

Minimums vary but are generally very low — typically 0.001 LTC or roughly $1 equivalent. BC.Game has one of the lowest minimums at 0.001 LTC. Stake and Roobet require roughly $1 equivalent in LTC.

Do Litecoin casinos charge deposit fees?

Most crypto casinos don't charge deposit fees — you only pay the network transaction fee (typically $0.01–0.05 for LTC). Some casinos charge withdrawal fees to cover their network costs, but these are usually minimal for Litecoin.

Is Litecoin better than Ethereum for casino deposits?

For pure transaction efficiency, Litecoin is often better. LTC fees are consistently low ($0.01–0.05), while Ethereum gas fees can spike during network congestion ($5–50+). However, Ethereum has broader DeFi integration and some casinos offer ETH-specific bonuses. For cost-conscious depositors, LTC wins.
