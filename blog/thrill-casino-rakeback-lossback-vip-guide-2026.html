2. The "70% Rakeback" Claim — What It Actually Means

This is the most important section in the guide. The "70% rakeback" headline is accurate but requires three clarifications to be useful:

Clarification 1: It's 70% of the house edge, not 70% of your losses

Rakeback is calculated against the casino's mathematical edge on each bet — not against your actual session loss or deposit. Here's what that looks like in practice:

Example — $200 bet on a slot with 4% house edge
House edge on this bet = $200 × 4% = $8.00
70% rakeback on that edge = $8 × 70% = $5.60 returned
Effective house edge after rakeback = 4% × (1 - 70%) = 1.2%

If you lost the full $200 bet: you get $5.60 back, not $140.

Rakeback meaningfully reduces the cost of play over volume, but it does not make a losing session whole. At 70% rakeback on a 4% house-edge game, your effective edge is 1.2% — still in the casino's favour.

Clarification 2: 70% is the top-tier ceiling, not the starting rate

New accounts begin at Base tier with rakeback, but the exact percentage at entry level is not publicly documented. The 70% figure is marketed as the "up to" maximum and publicly attributed to the highest VIP tiers (Diamond and above). Independent third-party reviewers report it corresponds to Thrill's highest tier — reachable only after extraordinary cumulative wagering (see the XP math section below).

No per-tier rakeback rates published: Unlike BC.Game (which publishes "3% at VIP 1, 12%+ at VIP 70+") or Gamdom (which shows progressive rates), Thrill does not list the rakeback percentage you receive at each of its 16 tiers. You can see your credits accumulate in real time, but you cannot easily calculate your effective rate. This is the most significant weakness of the Thrill rewards model from a player transparency standpoint.

Clarification 3: It's instant — no rollover required

One genuine advantage of Thrill's rakeback is that credits land in your Rewards balance immediately after each round closes, with no wagering requirement to withdraw. You must claim it via the Rewards tab (it doesn't auto-apply to your main balance), but once claimed, it's real balance — no 35x playthrough needed.

6. Lossback — Up to 10%, With Important Caveats

Lossback (also called cashback) at Thrill functions as a separate layer on top of instant rakeback. When you have a net loss position over a weekly or monthly cycle, Thrill offers a percentage of that loss back:

  • Maximum rate: Up to 10% of net losses
  • Cycles: Weekly and monthly — both run independently
  • Calculation basis: Net loss = (total amount wagered) - (total returns from winning bets) for the period. If you're net positive, there's nothing to claim.
  • Must be claimed manually: Within a limited window before the next cycle resets. Unclaimed lossback expires.
  • Per-tier rates not published: The exact lossback percentage at Base vs Gold vs Emerald is not publicly documented.
