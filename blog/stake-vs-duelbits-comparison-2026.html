Game Selection: Stake's Library is Bigger

Stake has more games. More house originals, more third-party slots, more live dealer tables, and more variety across the board. This isn't surprising — Stake has been operating since 2017 and has the scale to attract every major game provider.

Stake Originals are the highlight. Games like Crash, Dice, Plinko, Mines, Limbo, and Keno are smooth, provably fair, and have extremely low house edges (some as low as 1%). These house games are what Stake built its reputation on, and they're still best-in-class.

Duelbits has its own originals — including Crash, Dice, and a few others — and they work fine. But the selection is smaller, the interfaces feel slightly less polished, and the house edge on some games is marginally higher than Stake's equivalent. The Duelbits slot library is solid (2,500+ games from Pragmatic Play, Hacksaw Gaming, BGaming, and others), but Stake's is deeper with more providers represented.

Where Duelbits holds its own is on minimum bet sizes. You can wager as little as $0.01 on many Duelbits games, which is lower than Stake's minimums on some titles. If you're a micro-stakes player or just want to grind through wagering requirements slowly, Duelbits is more accessible.

Winner: Stake on overall selection and quality. Duelbits is fine but doesn't match the depth.

VIP & Rewards: Different Approaches

Stake's VIP programme is one of the best in crypto gambling. It's tiered (Bronze through Diamond and beyond), with each level unlocking better rakeback, reload bonuses, and personalised offers. The top tiers are invite-only and come with dedicated account managers, higher cashback, and exclusive promotions.

The key benefit for most players is the weekly/monthly bonuses. Active Stake players regularly receive reload offers and surprise bonuses based on their play volume. The system rewards consistent play over time, not just huge deposits.

Duelbits takes a different approach with a cashback/rakeback system that's more transparent upfront. You know what percentage you're getting back on every bet. The rates are competitive — sometimes higher than Stake's base VIP levels — and there are fewer tiers to climb through. Duelbits also runs regular promotions and giveaways.

The trade-off: Stake's top-tier VIP rewards are significantly more generous than anything Duelbits offers, but you need to wager substantial amounts to unlock them. For mid-volume players, Duelbits' straightforward cashback might actually deliver more value than Stake's lower VIP tiers.

💡 Reality check: VIP programmes are designed to keep you playing. The cashback sounds great until you calculate that you're losing more than you're getting back. Track your net results — not just your rewards. Use responsible limits regardless of which platform you're on.
