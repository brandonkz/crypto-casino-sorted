House Edge & Game Fairness

What does a 1% house edge actually mean in practice? It means for every $100 you wager on Shuffle originals, the expected house take is $1. On Roobet, that same $100 in wagers costs you $2–$4 in expected losses. Over time, especially for high-volume players, that difference compounds massively. If you're the kind of degen who grinds dice or mines for hours, Shuffle's edge literally saves you money.

Roobet uses provably fair systems for their original games, which is solid. Shuffle does the same. That said — and this is important — the house edge on originals doesn't apply to third-party slots or live casino games.
