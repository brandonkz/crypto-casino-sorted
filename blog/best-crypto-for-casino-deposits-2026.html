Detailed Breakdown by Cryptocurrency

🥇 Solana (SOL) — The Speed King

Solana has become the go-to for crypto casino power users, and for good reason. Sub-second finality means your deposit shows up almost before you switch tabs. Fees are essentially zero — we're talking fractions of a cent.

Network fees: ~$0.001 per transaction
Confirmation time: 2-5 seconds (1 confirmation typically required)
Casino support: Stake, BC.Game, Roobet, Duelbits, Rainbet, Gamdom + most Telegram casinos

The catch: Solana has had network congestion issues historically. While 2026 has been much more stable, there's a small risk of delayed transactions during peak periods. Also, if you need to convert SOL back to fiat, liquidity is thinner than BTC or ETH on some exchanges.

🥈 Litecoin (LTC) — The Reliable Workhorse

Litecoin isn't exciting. Nobody tweets about LTC anymore. But for casino deposits, it's quietly perfect: cheap, fast enough, and accepted literally everywhere.

Network fees: $0.01-0.05 per transaction
Confirmation time: 2.5 minutes per block (most casinos require 1-3 confirmations)
Casino support: Virtually every crypto casino accepts LTC

Why it works: LTC is the lowest-risk choice. The network has never gone down, fees have never spiked significantly, and every casino supports it. It's the Toyota Camry of crypto casino deposits — boring, reliable, gets the job done.
