How to Deposit Dogecoin at a Crypto Casino

If you've never deposited crypto at a casino before, here's the step-by-step:

  1. Create an account at your chosen casino. Most only need an email address.
  2. Go to the Deposit section and select Dogecoin (DOGE).
  3. Copy the deposit address — this is a unique DOGE wallet address generated by the casino for your account.
  4. Send DOGE from your personal wallet (Trust Wallet, Exodus, Ledger, exchange wallet, etc.) to the casino's deposit address.
  5. Wait 1-3 minutes for 1-3 network confirmations. Your balance will appear automatically.
Important: Always double-check the deposit address. Send a small test amount first if it's your first time. DOGE transactions are irreversible — if you send to the wrong address, the funds are gone. And never send DOGE to a Bitcoin or Ethereum address (different networks).
