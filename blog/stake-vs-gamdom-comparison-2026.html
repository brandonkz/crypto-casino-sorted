House Edge: Where Your Money Actually Goes

Let's start with the thing that matters most over the long run: house edge. This is the mathematical advantage the casino holds on every bet, and even small differences compound fast over thousands of wagers.

Stake Originals are provably fair and generally sit at a 1% house edge for games like Dice, Limbo, and Mines. Some games like Plinko can vary depending on the risk setting you choose. Stake is transparent about the math, and the provably fair system means you can verify every result. With 20+ original games, you've got variety — but the edge stays roughly in the same range.

Gamdom Originals are where things get interesting. Gamdom's Crash game runs at a 1% edge, same as Stake. But their Roulette and some other originals have been noted by players for offering slightly more favourable odds compared to industry standards. The difference isn't enormous — we're talking fractions of a percent — but for high-volume players, those fractions matter. Use our house edge calculator to see how this plays out over time.

For third-party slots and live dealer games, the house edge is set by the game provider (Pragmatic Play, Evolution, etc.), so it's effectively identical on both platforms. No advantage either way there.

Edge: Slight advantage to Gamdom on select original games. For slots and live casino, it's a wash.

VIP & Rakeback Programs: This Is Where It Gets Real
