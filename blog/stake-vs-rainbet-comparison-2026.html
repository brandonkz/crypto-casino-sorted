Provably Fair: Trust Through Transparency

Both Stake and Rainbet offer provably fair games — a cryptographic system that lets you verify every game result was genuinely random and not manipulated by the casino. This is one of the most important innovations in online gambling.

How it works: Before each bet, the casino generates a server seed (hidden) and you have a client seed (visible). After the bet, the server seed is revealed and you can verify that the result was mathematically determined by the combination of both seeds. No one — not even the casino — could have predicted or altered the outcome.

Stake's provably fair system is mature and well-documented. All of their original games (Dice, Mines, Plinko, Limbo, Crash, etc.) are provably fair, and the verification process is straightforward. Rainbet's original games also implement provably fair algorithms with similar transparency.

Important caveat: third-party slot games (from Pragmatic Play, Hacksaw, etc.) are not provably fair on either platform. These games use certified random number generators (RNG) audited by the game provider, not the cryptographic verification used by original games. This is standard across the industry — no crypto casino can make third-party slots provably fair.
