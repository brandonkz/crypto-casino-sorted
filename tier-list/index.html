How we rank the list

This page is intentionally opinionated, not neutral. Our editorial default gives extra weight to reward programs with readable math, cleaner withdrawal behavior, lower account friction, and fewer gimmicks hiding the real value. That means some brands with loud headline bonuses land lower than smaller operators with saner recurring value.

The tier list is also separate from a pure EV calculator. A casino can offer interesting bonus math and still land lower because its support, KYC handling, geo-restrictions, token settlement, or general trust profile makes it harder to recommend publicly. Visitors can disagree and publish their own build, but the default list still reflects our view.

  • S tier means we would defend the pick even if someone actually used the site after reading us.
  • A and B tiers are strong but depend more on your style: sportsbook use, reward tolerance, token appetite, or willingness to accept friction.
  • C and D tiers are not automatically scams. They are brands we would rank lower because the value, clarity, or trust profile is weaker.
  • Not Played is a real holding opinion. It means we are not willing to pretend certainty where we do not have it.

Shared links preserve order inside each tier. If a future operator is added without an editorial placement, the page leaves it in Unranked and flags the mismatch instead of guessing.
