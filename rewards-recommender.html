Method
Directional

Reward / edge-value estimate = modeled wager x selected game edge x active effective reward rate. The active rate starts with our conservative effective rate, then scales toward the published max rate as monthly wager rises for high-volume ladders such as Thrill, Duelbits and Cloudbet. By default, modeled wager is normal monthly play estimated from the player’s usual deposit value and minutes played per day. If the welcome-bonus toggle is enabled, verified bonus playthrough is added where available; unclear terms use a clearly labeled 30x fallback. For zero-edge operators, this is modeled avoided house-edge loss on eligible games, not a cash rebate. Casino order is based purely on estimated dollar value back / avoided loss. Trust, transparency, source status, friction, VIP depth, tier-match, and token risk are shown as context only, not ranking inputs. Affiliate status is not used in scoring.
