Rakeback Math: Edge, Wager and Contest Value

The quick conversion layer for comparing headline reward rates without confusing percent-of-edge with percent-of-wager.

Common Basis% of house edge
Cleanest Zero-Edge CaseDuel eligible originals
Contest ValueNot guaranteed rakeback

Why this chapter exists: crypto casino reward pages often put a large percentage in the headline, but the percentage usually applies to theoretical house edge, net loss, XP, or a temporary boost rather than the whole wager. A 5% house-edge rakeback rate on a 2% edge game returns about 0.1% of wager. A 50% house-edge rakeback rate on a 4% edge game returns about 2% of wager. That difference decides whether a program belongs in an EV comparison or only in a perks comparison.

Bonus Stack Attribution: Do Not Double Count Rewards

A source-aware way to separate base rakeback, rank cashback, calendar value, lossback, reloads, contests and welcome-bonus states before comparing operators.

Core RuleModel one basis at a time
Common TrapAdding every headline at face value
Best UseSource status and player fit

Why this matters: many crypto-casino reward pages show a stack of benefits, but the layers often measure different things. Rakeback can be a percentage of house edge, cashback can be rank-based or loss-based, reloads can depend on recent wager and results, and calendar rewards can delay or expire. The handbook should only add layers together when the source makes the basis, timing and eligibility clear enough to avoid double counting.
