Rakeback Math: Edge, Wager and Contest Value

The quick conversion layer for comparing headline reward rates without confusing percent-of-edge with percent-of-wager.

Common Basis% of house edge
Cleanest Zero-Edge CaseDuel eligible originals
Contest ValueNot guaranteed rakeback

Why this chapter exists: crypto casino reward pages often put a large percentage in the headline, but the percentage usually applies to theoretical house edge, net loss, XP, or a temporary boost rather than the whole wager. A 5% house-edge rakeback rate on a 2% edge game returns about 0.1% of wager. A 50% house-edge rakeback rate on a 4% edge game returns about 2% of wager. That difference decides whether a program belongs in an EV comparison or only in a perks comparison.
