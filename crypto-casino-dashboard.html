⚠ Data Methodology

This dashboard tracks on-chain deposits to known crypto casino wallet addresses on Ethereum mainnet (ETH). Wallet addresses are sourced from Arkham Intelligence and Etherscan labels. Data does not include deposits on other chains (BTC, SOL, TRON, BSC) or fiat deposits. Volume figures represent ETH deposits only and are a subset of total casino activity. Casino names are normalized (e.g. "Stake 11" → "Stake"). All USD values use the ETH price at the time of the transaction.
