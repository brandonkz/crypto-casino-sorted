How We Rank Casinos

Our rankings are determined by three objective factors:

  • Blockchain Data: We track actual deposits via public wallet addresses on Etherscan. Volume, transaction count, and whale behavior inform our analysis.
  • Reddit Sentiment: We manually aggregate community feedback from r/gambling, r/Stake, r/cryptogambling, and other relevant subreddits.
  • Verified Features: KYC requirements, withdrawal speeds, provably fair systems, VPN policies - all verified through testing or community reports.
Casinos cannot pay to improve their ranking. If a casino offers us a higher commission to rank them #1, we decline. Our rankings reflect data and community sentiment only.

What We Track (And What We Don't)

What we track:

  • Publicly labeled deposit wallets on Ethereum and BSC
  • Transaction volume and count (updated daily at 2 AM)
  • Average deposit sizes per casino
  • Whale activity (deposits over $10K)

What we DON'T track:

  • Fiat deposits (credit card, bank transfer, etc.)
  • Unlabeled or private wallets
  • Withdrawals (only deposits are publicly visible)
  • Other blockchains we haven't integrated yet (Solana, Tron, etc.)

Our data represents a sample of on-chain activity, not total casino volume. Take it as one data point among many.
