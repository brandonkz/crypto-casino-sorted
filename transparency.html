How We Rank Rewards

We score every casino's rewards program against five objective factors. The full breakdown lives on our How We Rank page, but in short:

  • Expected value: whether rakeback, cashback, lossback, bonuses and promotions have realistic value after wagering terms, game weighting and withdrawal restrictions.
  • Trust: visible terms, provably fair tools, public reputation, withdrawal complaints and consistency between marketing and the actual rules.
  • Clarity: clear reward formulas, bonus terms and KYC triggers score higher than vague promises or hidden thresholds.
  • Friction: we penalise withdrawal delays, confusing wallet flows, surprise verification and bonus lockups.
  • Depth: VIP progression, supported coins, game library and whether there's enough substance for repeat play.
Casinos cannot pay to improve their ranking. If a casino offers us a higher commission to rank them #1, we decline. Rankings reflect the math and the terms — nothing else.

How We Evaluate Rewards

What we do:

  • Read the actual rakeback, cashback and VIP terms — not the marketing headline
  • Model bonus value after wagering requirements and game weighting (see our Bonus Calculator)
  • Compare rakeback rates side by side across operators (see Rakeback Comparison)
  • Factor in withdrawal speed, KYC triggers and community sentiment from Reddit and Discord

What we DON'T do:

  • Rank casinos by who pays us the most
  • Republish casino marketing copy as if it were a review
  • Ignore the fine print — wagering requirements and reward caps are where the real value is decided

Reward terms change often and can vary by region. We verify them regularly, but always confirm the current offer directly with the casino before depositing.
